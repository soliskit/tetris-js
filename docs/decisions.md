# Decisions and limits

Current approved decisions with implementation checks still pending. Requirements define behavior; this table links the decision to its implementation and evidence. It is not a historical decision-log reconstruction.

| ID | Status | Decision and limits | Requirements | Implementation | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SAVE-1 | Pending work | One labeled record publishes a validated position and its availability atomically. Earlier raw-position saves are refused whole with bytes retained, without migration. Failed record replacement preserves the preceding record; successful withdrawal changes availability metadata while retaining the exact position string. Invalid raw content is retained unchanged. A local withdrawal lasts until that session completes a valid save. Cached old writers and concurrent read-modify-write races are not coordinated. | [STA-4, STA-6 and saving/withdrawal rules](requirements.md#saving-withdrawing-and-reading-a-game) | [session parser and record grammar](../public/game/session.js), [publication and withdrawal](../public/game/gameManager.js) | [record and validity regressions](../test/savedRecord.test.js), [independent semantic oracle](../test/semanticSave.test.js), [failure regressions](../test/gameManager.test.js), [browser saving regressions](../e2e/save-publication.spec.js), [serialization regressions](../e2e/serialization-failure.spec.js) |
| VALID-1 | Pending work | Saved scores have no divisibility restriction and retain their bounded stored domain; the in-session score domain is not narrowed to that ceiling. Full-row snapshots are permitted. Count16 is valid only paused/resting. Lowest-row history uses blocks of the current falling piece, can exceed its current occupied-row maximum, and restores exactly without missing-field defaults. | [B9, B11, B15, B16](requirements.md#valid-game-state) | [saved-content validation](../public/game/session.js), [score and piece-history handling](../public/game/gameManager.js) | [session validity regressions](../test/session.test.js), [history and score regressions](../test/savedRecord.test.js), [operation regressions](../test/gameManager.test.js) |
