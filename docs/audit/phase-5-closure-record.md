## What stays open

Four (F33, F34, F35, F37) carry defect-confirmation narratives in DECISIONS.md; their section 5.1 state (Confirmed Defect) is the crosswalk's proposed mapping, not an adopted status word. Eight have no section 5.1 mapping in these sources and keep their register states unchanged: F8 (Open - no decision made; a Mac-only key report on a platform the game does not support) and the seven Settled rows F1, F2, F10, F13, F14, F16 and F28a. The Settled rows are not one case: F10's earlier disposition is "Resolved, No Change" (the surviving mutant was harmless; no repair), F13 and F14 were test-coverage gaps that gained tests, and F1, F2, F16 and F28a record repairs. Reverification under this audit completed October 23, 2026 at its stated scope: two Node tests break the invariant check and the guard still catches and reports the fault (test/safety.test.js:213 and :224); browser corroboration covers a generic engine failure with separately injected console and draw refusals (e2e/engine-fault-notice.spec.js), not the Node throw routes; scripted fault injection, not an organic failure; browser viewports, not a physical device.
