# Phase 4 Work Families

The eight areas still needed before the audit phase can finish.

Phase 4 is closed, with five documented limits (D66 and D71 in DECISIONS.md). These eight work families preserve the earlier finite work ledger, updated for later detector decisions. A counterexample can stop its exact target, not the whole phase. No source-navigation count, case count or completed publication closes a family.

| Family | Work and remaining gates |
|---|---|
| State-space attacks | Reconcile independently justified valid/invalid partitions for dimensions, sparse cells, pieces, rotations, positions, queue, hold, bag, score, resources and flags. The current piece checks are implemented in [`isValidFallingPiece` and `isValidPieceList`](../../public/game/tetromino.js), called by the [engine monitor](../../public/game/gameManager.js). Earlier count-only queue and current-piece-fit source comparisons describe the pre-checker code, not the current detector. The semantic decisions are unchanged. [PR106](https://github.com/soliskit/tetris-js/pull/106)'s publication receipt remains to be independently reconciled; PR171 is not a substitute. See the [retained zero-cell evidence](zero-cell-current-piece-detector-disposition.md#retained-bounded-evidence) and [historical timer-handle evidence](timer-handle-detector-disposition.md#historical-evidence-stays-separate). Injected-boundary evidence does not establish ordinary malformed-piece creation or full R1. |
| Every writer | Bind each authoritative writer to preconditions, partial writes, persistence order and failure effects. Navigation lists are not an exhaustive writer ledger. Testing preservation of the last good save still needs [a prior save written by the game whose content and history have independently been shown valid](phase-3-current-method-support.md#held-evidence-bindings-and-honest-gaps), as recorded in [AUDIT F29](../../DECISIONS.md). |
| Every route | Reconcile direct entries and dependency failures with checks, safe stop, storage, timers and reporting. A protected neighboring route cannot discharge an unguarded entry. Correction preparation is not a reason to stop evidence review. See [New Game failure](phase-4-work-in-progress.md#confirmed-new-game-factory-failure) and [paused soft-drop evidence](phase-4-work-in-progress.md#paused-held-soft-drop-callback). |
| Outer layers | Keep drawing, controller reading, animation-frame registration, resize, visibility, touch and storage-event scopes separate. Notice, test signal and continued operation each need evidence. Invalid attempts stay invalid; native and physical-device gaps are not waived. See [paused soft-drop evidence](phase-4-work-in-progress.md#paused-held-soft-drop-callback) and the [separate replacement-archive limits](phase-4-work-in-progress.md#sources-coverage-and-remaining-limits). See the separate [drawing console report decision](drawing-console-report-policy.md). |
| Protection attacks | Retain scratch-only variant survival and semantic relevance. A selected surviving score-check variant does not establish a requirement violation merely by admitting a large integer allowed by the adopted score domain. Whole mutation adequacy remains open; the scratch attacks did not change shipped protections. The preserved `SCORE-ITEM12-ACTUAL-RESULT-v433.tar-fd153698.gz` contains the recorded process outcomes and results for one historical score-check variant. It does not validate the current test suite or the full QA-6 mutation campaign. |
| Independent expectations | Complete claim-linked expected-value and fixture provenance for important tests. Requirement navigation lanes are not the complete important-test universe. Coverage and agreement with the same validator cannot supply their own oracle. |
| Original candidate sequence | Reconcile F20, serialization, after-withdrawal New Game, failed reads, malformed predicates, gravity, legal clears, edited Hold, score mutation, drawing, scheduler and entry-wrapper targets separately. The narrated 3,738-lock trace still lacks exact harness, seed, bound and raw log. Findings do not settle broader saved-content or timing properties. |
| Cache and platform | Preserve the original cache attack classes with separate identity, order and publication arguments. Native install/reopen, physical timing and remaining device relations need evidence or a justified approved limit. See [cache deletion and reopened identity](phase-4-work-in-progress.md#cache-deletion-and-reopened-identity) and the separate replacement-archive limits in [Sources, coverage and remaining limits](phase-4-work-in-progress.md#sources-coverage-and-remaining-limits). Approved phone limits cover only both quick taps reaching the game, double-tap recovery, zoomed-reload recovery and exact cached phone-file identity. |

The per-writer, per-route and per-candidate inventory still needs reconciliation. Existing source dispositions and bounded historical evidence retain their own methods and limits. Findings, property certification and correction approval are separate. This ledger authorizes no production, permanent-test, model, CI, requirement or public-site change, and supplies no Phase 10 finish estimate.
